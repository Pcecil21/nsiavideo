NSIAVideo privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

NSIAVideo is a small script, run on our own computer, that files hockey videos sent to the team video inbox, nsiamedia123@gmail.com. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read and label): emails sent to nsiamedia123@gmail.com. For each email, that means the sender's address, the subject, the date, video attachments, and links to videos. It adds labels to mark each email as handled. It does not send, reply to, forward or delete email.
  • Google Drive (read-only): video files that senders have shared with nsiamedia123@gmail.com through a Drive link. It only downloads those files.

It accesses only the nsiamedia123@gmail.com account. It never accesses anyone else's Google account.

What it does with the data

Videos are saved into team folders in our own Google Drive and converted for showing in Score Vision. A local log records the sender's address, the subject, and where each video was saved, so problems can be fixed. That log stays on our computer.

What it does not do

We do not sell or rent this data. We do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models. We do not transfer it to anyone except as needed to show team videos in Score Vision. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and removing data

Videos and logs are kept for as long as they are useful to the teams. To have a video you sent removed, or for any question about this policy, email nsiamedia123@gmail.com.
