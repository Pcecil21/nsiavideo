NSIAVideo

Team video inbox for hockey games at NSIA

Coaches and parents email game videos to nsiamedia123@gmail.com. A script on our own computer reads that inbox, saves each video into the right team folder in our Google Drive, and converts it so it can be shown in Score Vision.

NSIAVideo is not a public service. Nobody else signs in to it, and it never accesses anyone else's Google account. It only reads the one inbox above.
